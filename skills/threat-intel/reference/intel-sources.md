## Live intelligence sources

**As of September 27, 2026.** Treat these as different kinds of evidence, not one interchangeable CVE feed: a KEV entry is evidence of exploitation; an advisory describes an affected product and fix; a public PoC is evidence of published code, not of successful exploitation; and a template is a candidate detection whose behavior still needs review ([CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog), [GitHub Advisory Database](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/security-advisories/global-advisories), [FIRST EPSS](https://www.first.org/epss/data), [Nuclei templates](https://github.com/projectdiscovery/nuclei-templates)).

In the tables, **speed** means how quickly a source tends to expose a newly disclosed issue; **reliability** means how strongly its entries support the claim being made. "Event-driven" means no fixed publication schedule was verified. Daily polling is an ingestion schedule for the program, not a claim about the publisher's cadence.
